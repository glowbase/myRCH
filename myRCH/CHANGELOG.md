# Changelog

All notable changes to myRCH. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/):

- **Major** (2.0.0): a big redesign, or a change that drops something people rely on.
- **Minor** (1.1.0): new features.
- **Patch** (1.0.1): fixes and small polish only.

The build number goes up by one for every build installed on a device or uploaded, and never goes back down. Version and build are set once, at project level (`MARKETING_VERSION` and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION`), and shared by the app, widgets and watch app.

The app shows this file under Settings > Version, so keep each entry to one line and write it for parents, not developers.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-04

The first numbered version, covering everything built since the project began on 25 September 2026.

### Home and Browse
- Home styled after the Health app, with the child's name, age and UR number, and a large-print UR sheet for check-in.
- Pinned sections you can reorder or hide with Edit Home: Highlights, Upcoming Visits, Recent Results, Medication, Immunisations, Health Goals and more.
- Highlights worked out on your iPhone: the next visit, doses to log, new or flagged results and unread messages.
- Search from Home and Browse that finds sections and individual records.
- Category chips and a large title on Home.

### Records
- Test results by month, with filters, normal-range graphs, trends over time, culture results and imaging reports.
- Medications with tidied names, brand-name banners and personal notes, and the option to remove medications you reported.
- Appointments with preparation steps, visit summaries, visitor maps, the clinician's details and rescheduling.
- Health summary, immunisations with per-dose details, growth charts against WHO and CDC percentiles, and health goals from the portal.
- Letters from the hospital, with a type filter and search.
- Messages with the care team, including bookmarks, archiving and replies with attachments.
- Medical ID with allergies, conditions and current medication on one page.

### Medication reminders
- Reminders at each dose time with Taken, Skip and Remind Me actions, and a follow-up if a dose isn't logged.
- Shared reminders with another parent through iCloud.
- Siri and Shortcuts to log the next dose.

### Widgets, Watch and Live Activities
- Widgets for the next dose, today's medication, the next visit, the UR number, allergies and what's new.
- Live Activities for a dose that's due and the day of a visit.
- Control Centre controls, and an Apple Watch app.

### Explain with Apple Intelligence
- Plain-language explanations of results, letters, medications, immunisations and growth, worked out on your iPhone.
- Questions to ask at a visit, visit recaps, help writing messages, a Home digest and a medical words glossary.

### Discover
- RCH News, Kids and Teen Health Info fact sheets and RCH podcasts, with alerts for new stories and fact sheets.

### Account and settings
- Sign in with the portal's two-step verification, and switch between the children on your account.
- Nicknames, colours and photos for each child.
- Personal Information: see and edit your email, mobile and work phone.
- Login & Security: see your password and two-step settings, and switch preview features and remembered devices.
- Lock myRCH with Face ID or Touch ID, and an option to keep portal data on your iPhone for quicker opening.
- Settings organised into Records, Health, Portal Account, App, Privacy and About.
- Version numbers and this changelog, under Settings > Version.
